Loom

A community-run AI lab.
Built by everyone.

What is Loom?

Loom is an open, community-driven AI lab where anyone can join and build.

Not a company.
Not a closed research group.
Not another passive AI server.

Loom exists for one thing:

People coming together to experiment, build, and push AI forward.

No gatekeeping.
No waiting for permission.
No centralized control.

If you have an idea, start building it.
If you want to contribute, join an experiment.

Why Loom Exists

Right now, AI is mostly shaped by a handful of companies and labs.

Most people only get to:

use the models
consume the products
adapt to the systems

Very few get to actually shape them.

Loom exists to change that.

We believe intelligence should not be built behind closed doors by a small group of organizations.

It should emerge from:

collaboration
experimentation
community
What Happens Inside Loom?

Loom is centered around experiments.

Anyone can:

propose an idea
start a project
contribute to existing work
form small build teams

Focus areas include:

Transformer architectures
Multi-agent systems
Training methods
Alignment experiments
CPU/GPU-efficient models
Interaction-based learning
Open-source tooling

But Loom is not restricted to any one direction.

If it's interesting and people want to build it, it belongs here.

Philosophy
Build > Talk

Ideas matter.
Execution matters more.

Open by Default

Experiments, progress, and failures should be visible.

No Gatekeeping

You don't need permission to start building.

Community Over Hierarchy

Influence comes from contribution, not titles.

Experiment Fast

Ship rough ideas. Improve publicly.

Current Goals
Build original AI systems and experiments
Explore alternatives to centralized AI development
Create an ecosystem of builders and researchers
Make AI development more accessible
Push unconventional ideas forward

willingness to experiment
